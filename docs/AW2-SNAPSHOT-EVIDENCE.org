#+title: Milestone 2 structured-snapshot scenario evidence

This index records how to reproduce and inspect each structured-snapshot
scenario, the observed result, and where its non-Git evidence is stored. The
supported ROM SHA-256 is
=ef3cc89273f9df88020f07751ea6306b25c39df01893822fe431550eedf9b134=.
Screenshots, save states, and RAM dumps stay outside the repository; their
source hashes and capture metadata identify the exact historical checkpoints.

The supplied movement, purchase, capture, and turn-change source save states
were not copied into the research folders. Their hashes remain in each
=metadata.json= or =manifest.json=, and their screenshots and full RAM captures
are preserved. To replay one of those exact states, use a source save matching
the recorded hash; otherwise recreate the scenario from the steps below and
record a new source hash. Combat also preserves generated =.ss0= branch states.

* Movement and associated supply observation
- Procedure: restore the before-move APC checkpoint identified by the
  =aw2.ss2= hash in =/tmp/aw2-clean-supply-research/metadata.json=. Select the
  Red APC at candidate coordinate =(7,5)=, move it one tile north to =(7,4)=,
  and choose Wait. Capture the settled state and compare it with the after
  checkpoint (=aw2.ss3=).
- Manual evidence: =state2.png= and =state3.png= in
  =/tmp/aw2-clean-supply-research/= show the APC before/after; the HUD and
  map location agree with the interpreted coordinates.
- Result: both snapshots validate as live player turns. The APC's byte +6
  changes 68 to 67; the adjacent Infantry's changes 94 to 99, and the Md
  Tank's changes 45 to 50. The map, properties, and player records are
  unchanged. This supports a movement-associated supply effect but does not
  isolate its trigger radius or timing.
- Recheck: run =python /tmp/aw2-clean-supply-research/compare_movement_snapshots.py=.
  Findings and field limits: [[file:AW2-MAP-RESEARCH.org::*Validated snapshot comparison][movement comparison]].

* Combat damage and unit destruction
- Procedure: restore =red-ready.ss0= (frame 19421) from
  =/tmp/aw2-map-research-four/=, with the Red Tank at =(1,5)= and adjacent
  Infantry targets. For the nonlethal branch, select Fire on the target at
  =(2,5)= and let the animation settle. For destruction, restore the archived
  =forecast.ss0= in =/tmp/aw2-combat-lifecycle-research/=, use the 44-HP Infantry,
  continue the attack trace,
  and sample the zero-health frame, type-clear frame, and settled cleanup.
- Manual evidence: paired screenshots for before, damage settled, zero HP,
  and settled destruction are in =/tmp/aw2-combat-lifecycle-research/=. The

- Result: the Tank and target health changes match the screenshot. During
  destruction, the occupied zero-HP record is rejected; after its type clears,
  the active count drops by one; live decoding resumes after cleanup. No
  damage formula or unit action-byte meaning is inferred.

  =python /tmp/aw2-combat-lifecycle-research/compare_structured_combat_snapshots.py=.
  Input traces, generated branches, and findings are described in
  [[file:AW2-LIFECYCLE-RESEARCH.org::*Structured snapshot comparison for combat damage and destruction][the combat comparison]].

* Unit purchase after destruction
- Procedure: for the first sequence, start from the hashed pre-attack state
  in =/tmp/aw2-unit-reuse-research/metadata.json=, destroy the low-health Red
  Infantry, then purchase an Infantry at the Red base. For the second, repeat
  with the source state in =/tmp/aw2-unit-reuse-followup/metadata.json= and
  buy the same unit after destruction. Capture before, destroyed, and
  purchased states.
- Manual evidence: the state screenshots in both folders show the unit
  disappearing, the purchase at the base, and the HUD funds.
- Result: funds fall by 1,000 in each purchase. In the first sequence the
  allocator chooses an earlier empty slot; in the repeat it reuses the slot
  just freed. All snapshots validate, and the map and property arrays stay
  unchanged.
- Recheck: run
  =python /tmp/aw2-unit-reuse-research/compare_purchase_snapshots.py=.
  The two sequences and slot limits are described in
  [[file:AW2-MAP-RESEARCH.org::*Validated structured comparison of purchase checkpoints][the purchase comparison]].
