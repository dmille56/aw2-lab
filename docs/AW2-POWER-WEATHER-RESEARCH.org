#+TITLE: AW2 Power Meter and Weather Research

* Result and scope

Date: 2026-10-25. This study uses the checksum-verified US revision-0 ROM
=ef3cc89273f9df88020f07751ea6306b25c39df01893822fe431550eedf9b134=
and pinned headless mGBA =fd9dc5c2cbf234365068855851107e762fc6211b=.
The baseline study uses eleven coherent full-RAM snapshots to compare Andy
and Max's retained activation traces, a newly executed Sami regular Power, and
separate Clear/Rain Rules branches. Follow-ups trace Olaf's Blizzard and
Drake's Typhoon through their next-turn weather resets. All four supported
structures validate in every sample. Command menus and power presentations
remain unavailable for live decoding; settled player turns pass the existing
lifecycle gate.

The study verifies meter storage, spending, and low activation-counter values,
plus current Clear/Rain/Snow weather and the Olaf Blizzard and Drake Typhoon
weather overrides through expiry. New paired checkpoints distinguish an attack
without counterfire from one with counterfire. A trace of the original ROM's
combat-charge routine explains all four observed meter deltas: each side gets
its own damage-value term plus half the opposing term, using whole-HP damage
and a meter valuation coefficient from the ROM unit table. Static ROM values
for the nonzero Kanbei, Hachi, and Colin adjustments are mapped; runtime
verification now covers Kanbei's inactive +20% and Colin's inactive -20%, as
well as Hachi's inactive, Power, and Super modifiers. ROM analysis resolves
fractional meter rounding for valid unit and CO values. Natural near-cap
evidence and the individual meanings of weather mode/neighbor bytes remain
open. Runtime testing above counter 10 and at counter saturation is deferred
by user request. The structured state decoder now publishes the verified
subset: active Power, nonnegative raw meter charge, use counter values through
10, and current weather. Negative meter readings and counters above 10 remain
unclassified. The state API remains a separate task.

* Verified fields and raw observations

Use =R(k) = 0x02023284 + 0x3C*k= for participating player slots 1..4.
Select the supported ROM profile before following its layout literals.

| Address | Field | Representation | Verified interpretation and limit |
|---------+-------+----------------+-----------------------------------|
| =R(k)+0x1E= | Active Power | =u8= | 0 inactive, 1 Power, 2 Super; supported by Andy, Max, Sami, Olaf, and Drake |
| =R(k)+0x20= | Meter charge | 32-bit LE; signed ROM comparisons | Nonnegative raw charge verified in these samples; negative values are not validated charge |
| =R(k)+0x25= | Power-use scaling counter | =u8= | Increments before spending; transitions 0 to 1 and 1 to 2 verified |
| =0x03003FEC= | Current weather | =u8= | 0 Clear, 1 Snow, and 2 Rain match controlled screenshots and power checkpoints |
| =0x03003FED= | Weather transition mode | =u8= | 0 in the settled Clear Rules battle; 1 across runtime Random Clear→Rain→Clear and Clear→Snow→Clear cycles; 3 in fixed Rain and Snow Rules starts. Mode 2 holds current non-Clear weather in the resolver, but no setup use is identified. |

Other neighboring fields are retained as raw observations:

- =R(k)+0x1F= holds the selected activation kind in these paths. It may retain
  1/2 while =+0x1E= is zero; it must not replace the active-Power flag.
- =R(k)+0x24= is a cached readiness value. The ROM updater can raise it to
  1/2 on the corresponding thresholds and activation clears it. It is not an
  independent command-availability predicate.
- =0x03003FEE= stores the weather resolver's computed result during turn
  updates; =0x03003FEF= is the target weather read by that resolver. The rule
  that selects a nonzero target and the cached result's full lifetime are
  still unknown. See [[*Rules-screen weather choices and settled weather bytes][the weather resolver trace]].

* Meter readers, thresholds, and spending

Original-ROM routines =0x08044080= and =0x08044094= write/read a word at
=R(k)+0x20=. The charge-adder at =0x080440E0= checks that CO Powers are
enabled, ignores an active Power or a negative meter, and clamps positive
growth to the Super threshold. The combat-event formula and its verification
against the new checkpoints are described below; the runtime effect of
nonzero CO adjustments remains open.

The per-star charge routine at =0x0804419C= reads the byte at =R(k)+0x25=
through =0x08044374=. For the observed counters 0, 1, and 2, its results are
9,000, 10,800, and 12,600 raw units per star. The formula on this branch is
=9000 * (100 + 20*n) / 100=.

The threshold routines at =0x080441D4= and =0x08044208= multiply that unit
by words from =0x085D3DD0 + 0x104*co_id + 0x0C/0x10=. These are the normal
star count and total Super star count in the tested CO rows: Andy/Max have
3/6; Sami has 3/8; Drake has 4/7. Do not interpret the Super value as only
the additional stars beyond normal Power.

=0x0804438C= increments the use counter through =0x08044354=, then invokes
the spending routine =0x080440A8=. Spending subtracts the threshold for the
updated counter and clamps negative results to zero. The controlled/retained
comparisons agree with that order:

| CO / activation | Frames before -> after | Charge before -> after | Counter before -> after | Deduction threshold |
|-----------------+------------------------+------------------------+-------------------------+---------------------|
| Andy Power | 128189 -> 128191 | 41,650 -> 3,850 | 1 -> 2 | 37,800 |
| Max Super | 10926 -> 10976 | 54,000 -> 0 | 0 -> 1 | 64,800, clamped at zero |
| Sami Power | 11464 -> 12458 | 72,000 -> 39,600 | 0 -> 1 | 32,400 |
| Olaf Power | 12442 -> 13808 | 63,000 -> 30,600 | 0 -> 1 | 32,400 |
| Drake Super | 61809 -> 62103 | 63,000 -> 0 | 0 -> 1 | 75,600, clamped at zero |

* Runtime attack, counterfire, and cap checks

Three supplied checkpoints form a consecutive two-attack sequence on a
two-player map. Their restored frames are =68746=, =69392=, and =69664=;
players 3 and 4 are inactive. Player 1 is Andy (CO ID 1), and player 2 has
CO ID 10. Across both transitions, only one unit record changes in each army.

| Checkpoint pair | Player 1 meter | Player 2 meter | Red target record | Blue attacker record | Result |
|-----------------+----------------+----------------+-------------------+---------------------+--------|
| =aw2.ss1= -> =aw2.ss2= | 7,500 -> 11,500 (+4,000) | 15,000 -> 17,000 (+2,000) | =0x020226C0=, byte +4: =0x64 -> 0x2C= (100 -> 44) | =0x02022A20=, byte +1: =0 -> 1=; byte +4: =0xE4 -> 0x64= (low 7 bits remain 100) | No counterfire: the Blue attacker remains at 100 health |
| =aw2.ss2= -> =aw2.ss3= | 11,500 -> 15,900 (+4,400) | 17,000 -> 19,800 (+2,800) | =0x020226CC=, byte +4: =0xE4 -> 0x2D= (low 7 bits: 100 -> 45) | =0x02022A2C=, byte +1: =0 -> 1=; byte +4: =0xE4 -> 0x58= (low 7 bits: 100 -> 88) | Counterfire: the Blue attacker loses 12 health points |

These observations support reading health from the low seven bits of byte +4:
the Blue attacker's =0xE4= is 100 health with bit 7 set, while the no-counter
case changes it to =0x64= without changing health. In the counterfire case it
changes to =0x58=, or 88 health. The two Red targets lose 56 and 55 raw health
points respectively. This confirms the distinction between the supplied
attack-only and counterfire examples.

The changing Red and Blue records both have type =0x0E=; their displayed unit
names are not verified by this comparison. Online
guides describe full-value charge for damage received and half-value charge
for damage dealt, using whole HP rather than fractional health. The original
ROM explains the apparent mismatch: it uses whole-HP loss and its meter-value
coefficient, not nominal shop price and fractional stored-HP loss.

At =0x08041C32=, the combat-charge routine builds a weighted term for each
combatant. It converts nonzero low-seven-bit health to a whole-HP bucket using
=((health - 1) / 10) + 1=, looks up the unit's meter-value coefficient at
=0x085D5ABC + 0x5C*unit_type + 6=, and applies a CO adjustment through
=sub_08043270=. The coefficient for unit type =0x0E= at
=0x085D5ABC + 0x5C*0x0E + 6= is =800=; this is the meter coefficient, not
the nominal shop price. The adjustment words for Andy (CO ID 1) and the other
participant (CO ID 10) with type =0x0E= are zero in these states, so both use
coefficient =800=.
The checkpoints show Red losing five displayed HP in each attack and Blue
losing one displayed HP in the counterfire. The resulting full-value terms
are Red =5*800= = 4,000 and Blue =1*800= = 800.

Instructions at =0x08041CAA= through =0x08041CC4= divide each term by two using
integer division, then add it to the other side's full term. If =R= is Red's
term and =B= is Blue's, Red receives =R + floor(B/2)= and Blue receives
=B + floor(R/2)=:

| Pair | Red term R | Blue term B | Predicted Red | Observed | Predicted Blue | Observed |
|------+------------+-------------+---------------+----------+----------------+----------|
| =aw2.ss1= -> =aw2.ss2= | 4,000 | 0 | 4,000 | 4,000 | 2,000 | 2,000 |
| =aw2.ss2= -> =aw2.ss3= | 4,000 | 800 | 4,400 | 4,400 | 2,800 | 2,800 |

This explains the apparent mismatch: the routine uses whole-HP damage terms
weighted by the ROM's meter coefficient, not fractional stored-HP changes
multiplied by nominal purchase prices. The online full/half rule matches the
verified routine for Infantry and unit type =0x0E=. The tested Andy, Sami,
and CO ID 10 combinations have zero adjustment words; values for nonzero CO
adjustments are mapped below, but their runtime effects remain to be checked. External
references are GameSpot's AW2 walkthrough
([[https://www.gamespot.com/articles/advance-wars-2-black-hole-rising-walkthrough/1100-6072205/][CO meter explanation and example]]), the Wars Wiki's
[[https://warswiki.org/wiki/Advance_Wars_2:_Black_Hole_Rising][AW2 unit data]] and
[[https://warswiki.org/wiki/Hachi][Hachi entry]], and the AWBW
[[https://awbw.fandom.com/wiki/CO][CO meter reference]].

The ROM coefficient lookup provides a second cross-check of CO adjustments.
Infantry type =0x01= has base coefficient =100= and unit type =0x0E= has
=800=. The adjustment words read by =sub_08043270= for the
sampled IDs Andy (1), Sami (4), and the other participant (10) are zero in

coefficients. Separately, controlled roster states identify Hachi as CO ID

and Colin (16) have nonzero entries. These IDs are independently mapped by
the controlled CO-selection states in [[file:AW2-MAP-RESEARCH.org::*Finding: additional CO setup and battle IDs in slot 1][the CO roster research]]. The ROM table at
=0x085D3DD0= contains the adjustment entries. Function
=sub_08043270= reads a signed little-endian halfword at
=0x085D3DD0 + 0x4C + 4*(65*CO_ID + 17*active_power)=, and
=sub_08042C9C= calculates =base * (100 + adjustment) / 100= using integer
division. The adjustment is selected by CO ID and the active-Power byte at
=R(k)+0x1E=; it does not depend on unit type. Values for inactive,
Power, and Super states are:

* Meter-term rounding

The combat routine applies a CO adjustment to the unit's meter coefficient
using integer division in =sub_08042C9C=. In the supported ROM, the 24 valid
unit-type rows (=0x01..0x18=) have a zero or a multiple-of-100 coefficient at
=0x085D5ABC + 0x5C*unit_type + 6=. The supported CO adjustments are 0, +20,
-10, -20, and -50 percent, all multiples of 10 percent. Consequently every
adjusted coefficient is an integer multiple of 10; multiplying by a whole-HP
loss keeps each combat term a multiple of 10, and the opposing half-term is a
multiple of 5. The integer divisions therefore discard no fractional raw
meter units for valid unmodified game data. This resolves the earlier
fractional-rounding question statically; a runtime checkpoint cannot exercise
a fractional case without altered or invalid unit/CO table data.

* Target field writer trace

The resolver at =0x08035170= only reads =+0x2F=; turn update
=sub_08042B9C= stores its result at =+0x2E=, and update callback
=sub_080351F0= compares =+0x2E= with current weather at =+0x2C=. The battle
weather-state initializer =0x08034780= writes zero to =+0x2F=, then clears
=+0x2C..+0x2E=. The generic record-byte loader has separate handlers for
=+0x2C=, =+0x2D=, =+0x2E=, and =+0x2F= at
=0x08071854..0x08071898=; the final handler copies the input record's byte
into =+0x2F=. Settled fixed Rain and Snow battles have =+0x2F= values 2 and 1,
respectively, confirming that setup can load a nonzero target from a record.
This is a load/initialization path, not evidence of a runtime weather
transition writer. Small mode setters at =0x08035538=,
=0x08035548=, and =0x08035558= write mode values 1, 2, and 0 respectively.
The generic loader can also load mode 3 from a record. The caller or data
that chooses mode 2 remains unidentified. The nonzero setup target source is
identified as the record-byte loader; a battle-time writer that changes the
target remains unidentified.

A full-ROM byte scan found no direct branch or aligned function-pointer
reference to the mode-2 setter at =0x08035548=. The byte sequence for its ROM
address appears once at an unaligned location (=0x080A0522=), inside data; it
does not establish a callback or event reference. This leaves a data-driven
invocation possible, but unverified. Startup code at =0x08000FB8= also writes
1 to offsets =+0x2E= and =+0x2F= through an EWRAM object pointer. The pointer's
global is =0x0200B0B0= and is null in the captured settled-battle EWRAM image;
the object has not been linked to the live IWRAM weather record. Treat this
as an initialization lead, not evidence of an in-battle target change. A
separate apparent =+0x2F= store at =0x08000D30= resolves to a table-based
address rather than the weather record and is not a weather writer.

The synchronized turn helper =sub_08035080= updates scratch counters at
=0x03004493 + active_player= and reports whether participating players have
matching counters. Helper =sub_080350E4= clears those counters and sets the
timer at =0x03004490= to 30; =sub_08035124= invokes it when passed a nonzero
weather differing from current. These helpers gate a change to the resolved
weather, not a write to the target. In captured Random and CO-power cycles,
=+0x2F= stays 0; the fixed Rain and Snow starts instead load their nonzero
targets during setup. No battle-time target write has been identified in the
traced resolver, turn-update, or CO-power paths. A data-driven event or other
writer outside those paths remains possible and unverified.

Turn-update routine =sub_08042B9C= stores the resolver's returned weather in
=+0x2E=. Routine =sub_080351F0= reads that byte, compares it with current
weather, and triggers a related update callback when they differ. Thus =+0x2E=
is a cached resolver result in these paths; its full lifetime/purpose is not
known. In settled fixed-weather starts, =+0x2E= and =+0x2F= match current
weather; both are zero under Clear. The earlier Rain branch remains at
=[2,3,2,2]= in its archived samples. Olaf and Drake override traces show =+0x2E= temporarily
matching CO-forced Snow/Rain during the opponent's turn while =+0x2F= stays
zero. The code establishes the target behavior of =+0x2F=, but not the
higher-level rule that sets it.

The resolver's scheduling helper =sub_08035080= increments a per-player counter
at =0x03004493 + active_player= and waits until all participating players'
counters match before allowing a target update. The runtime cycle below shows
these adjacent per-player counter bytes progressing as the two players finish
their turns, and the timer byte at =0x03004490= at 30 during the transition.
The exact counter initialization/reset rules and the higher-level condition
that changes the target remain unresolved.
