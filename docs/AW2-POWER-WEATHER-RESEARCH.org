#+TITLE: AW2 Power Meter and Weather Research

* Result and scope

Date: 2026-10-25. This study uses the checksum-verified US revision-0 ROM
=ef3cc89273f9df88020f07751ea6306b25c39df01893822fe431550eedf9b134=
and pinned headless mGBA =fd9dc5c2cbf234365068855851107e762fc6211b=.
The baseline study uses eleven coherent full-RAM snapshots to compare Andy
and Max's retained activation traces, a newly executed Sami regular Power, and
separate Clear/Random Rules branches. Follow-ups trace Olaf's Blizzard and
Drake's Typhoon through their next-turn weather resets. All four supported
structures validate in every sample. Command menus and power presentations
remain unavailable for live decoding; settled player turns pass the existing
lifecycle gate.

The study verifies meter storage, spending, and low activation-counter values,
plus current Clear/Rain/Snow weather and the Olaf Blizzard and Drake Typhoon
weather overrides through expiry. Combat charge earnings, high-counter
behavior, and weather-rule interactions remain open. These findings extend
the documented decoding subset; a typed state decoder and state API remain
separate tasks.

* Verified fields and raw observations

Use =R(k) = 0x02023284 + 0x3C*k= for participating player slots 1..4.
Select the supported ROM profile before following its layout literals.

| Address | Field | Representation | Verified interpretation and limit |
|---------+-------+----------------+-----------------------------------|
| =R(k)+0x1E= | Active Power | =u8= | 0 inactive, 1 Power, 2 Super; supported by Andy, Max, Sami, Olaf, and Drake |
| =R(k)+0x20= | Meter charge | 32-bit LE; signed ROM comparisons | Nonnegative raw charge verified in these samples; negative values are not validated charge |
| =R(k)+0x25= | Power-use scaling counter | =u8= | Increments before spending; transitions 0 to 1 and 1 to 2 verified |
| =0x03003FEC= | Current weather | =u8= | 0 Clear, 1 Snow, and 2 Rain match controlled screenshots and power checkpoints |
| =0x03003FED= | Weather rule/mode | =u8= | 0 for the Clear Rules branch and 3 for Random; modes 1/2 are not identified from these UI comparisons |

Other neighboring fields are retained as raw observations:

- =R(k)+0x1F= holds the selected activation kind in these paths. It may retain
  1/2 while =+0x1E= is zero; it must not replace the active-Power flag.
- =R(k)+0x24= is a cached readiness value. The ROM updater can raise it to
  1/2 on the corresponding thresholds and activation clears it. It is not an
  independent command-availability predicate.
- =0x03003FEE..0x03003FEF= participate in weather selection. Preserve them
  as raw bytes until their complete lifecycle is checked.

* Meter readers, thresholds, and spending

Original-ROM routines =0x08044080= and =0x08044094= write/read a word at
=R(k)+0x20=. The charge-adder at =0x080440E0= checks that CO Powers are
enabled, ignores an active Power or a negative meter, and clamps positive
growth to the Super threshold. This identifies the storage and clamping path;
the formula converting individual combat events into charge is not established.

The per-star charge routine at =0x0804419C= reads the byte at =R(k)+0x25=
through =0x08044374=. For the observed counters 0, 1, and 2, its results are
9,000, 10,800, and 12,600 raw units per star. The formula on this branch is
=9000 * (100 + 20*n) / 100=.

The threshold routines at =0x080441D4= and =0x08044208= multiply that unit
by words from =0x085D3DD0 + 0x104*co_id + 0x0C/0x10=. These are the normal
star count and total Super star count in the tested CO rows: Andy/Max have
3/6; Sami has 3/8; Drake has 4/7. Do not interpret the Super value as only
the additional stars beyond normal Power.

=0x0804438C= increments the use counter through =0x08044354=, then invokes
the spending routine =0x080440A8=. Spending subtracts the threshold for the
updated counter and clamps negative results to zero. The controlled/retained
comparisons agree with that order:

| CO / activation | Frames before -> after | Charge before -> after | Counter before -> after | Deduction threshold |
|-----------------+------------------------+------------------------+-------------------------+---------------------|
| Andy Power | 128189 -> 128191 | 41,650 -> 3,850 | 1 -> 2 | 37,800 |
| Max Super | 10926 -> 10976 | 54,000 -> 0 | 0 -> 1 | 64,800, clamped at zero |
| Sami Power | 11464 -> 12458 | 72,000 -> 39,600 | 0 -> 1 | 32,400 |
| Olaf Power | 12442 -> 13808 | 63,000 -> 30,600 | 0 -> 1 | 32,400 |
| Drake Super | 61809 -> 62103 | 63,000 -> 0 | 0 -> 1 | 75,600, clamped at zero |

The Andy, Max, Olaf, and Drake charge deductions occur while active status is
still zero and the lifecycle is =BATTLE_POWER=. Their settled observations at
frames 129003, 11623, 14140, and 62946 have active status 1, 2, 1, and 2
respectively. Sami settles with active status 1 at frame 12458. A zero active
status during the presentation therefore does not imply that no activation has
begun.

The static per-star routine has a separate branch for counters above 9 that
returns 18,000. That branch and counters 3..9 have not been exercised here;
do not extrapolate the runtime-verified progression or publish high-counter
thresholds as verified. The counter incrementer also has a static saturation
check at 255; no such runtime state was observed.

* Clear and Rain weather comparison

Both branches begin at the same retained Rules checkpoint, with only the
Weather choice changed. The Clear branch is sampled at frame 73552; the
Random branch at frame 73559. Screenshots show a normal Clear palette in the
first and gray water with diagonal rain streaks in the second.

| Rules choice | Current byte =0x03003FEC= | Mode byte =0x03003FED= | Neighbor bytes =0x03003FEE..EF= |
|--------------+---------------------------+------------------------+---------------------------------|
| Clear | 0 | 0 | 0, 0 |
| Random, producing Rain | 2 | 3 | 2, 2 |

Map dimensions, graphical tiles, terrain/owner cells, and the property table
are byte-identical across these two settled battles. The weather appearance
does not require a different terrain layout. Both pass live snapshot gating.

Original =0x0803504C= stores an effect's weather value at the configuration
base plus =0x2C=, and =0x08035020= uses that value to select the render palette.
=0x08035170= reads the current weather and mode separately. This supports
the distinction between a current condition and the Rules setting. A retained
Andy battle also has mode 1 while current weather is 0; this study assigns no
UI meaning to mode 1. The Olaf Blizzard sequence below verifies weather value
1 as Snow.

* Olaf Blizzard override and expiry

Five supplied checkpoints identify player 1 as Olaf (battle CO ID 3) and
player 2 as Max (ID 2). They show Clear before activation, the Blizzard
presentation, settled Snow, Snow during Max's turn, then Clear when Olaf next
regains control. The screenshots agree with the current-weather byte at
=0x03003FEC=. Olaf's regular Power also spends 32,400 meter units: the charge
falls from 63,000 to 30,600 as the use counter increments from 0 to 1. The
active-Power byte is still zero during the presentation, then becomes 1 after
the effect settles.

| Checkpoint | Frame | Day / active player | Phase | Current weather | Olaf active Power | Charge / uses |
|------------+-------+---------------------+-------+-----------------+-------------------+--------------|
| Before Blizzard | 12442 | 5 / Olaf | Player turn | 0 Clear | 0 | 63,000 / 0 |
| Blizzard presentation | 13808 | 5 / Olaf | Power presentation | 0 Clear | 0 | 30,600 / 1 |
| Blizzard settled | 14140 | 5 / Olaf | Player turn | 1 Snow | 1 | 30,600 / 1 |
| Max's turn | 14650 | 5 / Max | Player turn | 1 Snow | 1 | 30,600 / 1 |
| Olaf next turn | 14992 | 6 / Olaf | Player turn | 0 Clear | 0 | 30,600 / 1 |

Frame-by-frame replay brackets both field updates. Starting from the supplied
Blizzard-presentation state, the active-Power byte is 1 at frame 13964 while
weather remains Clear through frame 13995; weather first becomes Snow at
frame 13996. To cross the expiry, replay Max's End command from the supplied
Max-turn state. Olaf's active-Power byte clears at frame 14697 as day 6 starts,
but Snow remains through frame 14765. Current weather first returns to Clear
at frame 14766. The lifecycle is still blocked from live decoding at that
point; the supplied settled checkpoint at frame 14992 is a valid player turn.
Thus the weather reset occurs during the transition, after the CO active-Power
flag clears and before live decoding resumes.

The neighbor bytes at =0x03003FED..0x03003FEF= are retained in the external
trace without assigning new meanings. In particular, =+0x2= is 1 in the
opponent-turn checkpoint and 0 in the adjacent samples; its role is unresolved.
All twelve documented snapshots pass the four structural checks, including
the animation and transition samples where live decoding is correctly blocked.
The full per-sample RAM, screenshots, source hashes, and validation results are
in =olaf-weather-trace.json= under =/tmp/aw2-weather-power-research/=. The
frame-boundary checkpoints are named =olaf-blizzard-n156=, =n187=, and =n188=
for activation, and =olaf-expiry-4=, =olaf-expiry-72=, and =olaf-turn-n73= for
the next-turn transition.

* Drake Typhoon override and expiry

Seven supplied checkpoints identify player 1 as Drake (battle CO ID 9) and
player 2 as Max (ID 2). They show Clear before Typhoon, the selected Super
command and presentation, settled Rain, Rain during Max's turn, then Clear on
Drake's next turn. The screenshot and current-weather byte at =0x03003FEC=
agree. Drake's Super spends 63,000 available meter units, increments the use
counter from 0 to 1, and leaves the meter at zero. The ROM CO row has 7 Super
stars; at counter 1 the 75,600-unit threshold is clamped to zero. The active
Power byte remains zero during the presentation and is 2 after settlement.

| Checkpoint | Frame | Day / active player | Screen | Current weather | Drake active Power | Charge / uses |
|------------+-------+---------------------+-------+-----------------+--------------------+--------------|
| Before Typhoon | 61809 | 5 / Drake | Player turn | 0 Clear | 0 | 63,000 / 0 |
| Super selected | 61964 | 5 / Drake | Command menu | 0 Clear | 0 | 63,000 / 0 |
| Typhoon presentation | 62103 | 5 / Drake | Power presentation | 0 Clear | 0 | 0 / 1 |
| Typhoon splash | 62428 | 5 / Drake | Power presentation | 0 Clear | 0 | 0 / 1 |
| Typhoon settled | 62946 | 5 / Drake | Player turn | 2 Rain | 2 | 0 / 1 |
| Max's turn | 63342 | 5 / Max | Player turn | 2 Rain | 2 | 0 / 1 |
| Drake next turn | 63765 | 6 / Drake | Player turn | 0 Clear | 0 | 0 / 1 |

Frame-boundary replays show the active-Super byte is already 2 at frame 62748
while weather is still Clear. Clear remains through frame 62751; Rain first
appears at frame 62752. To trace expiry, open Max's command menu from the
supplied Max-turn checkpoint, select End, and confirm. Drake's active-Super
byte remains 2 with Rain at frame 63388. At frame 63389, as day 6 begins, the
Power byte is 0 but Rain remains. Rain is present through frame 63457 and the
weather first returns to Clear at frame 63458. Live decoding is still blocked
there and has resumed by frame 63685; the supplied settled checkpoint at frame
63765 also validates. As with
Blizzard, the weather resets after the active-Power flag clears and before
live decoding resumes.

The neighboring byte =0x03003FEE= is 2 in the opponent-turn checkpoint and 0
in adjacent source checkpoints; its meaning remains unknown. All fifteen
documented snapshots pass the four structural checks. Full RAM, screenshots,
source hashes, and lifecycle results are in =drake-weather-trace.json= under
=/tmp/aw2-weather-power-research/=. It names the boundary samples
=drake-typhoon-n320=, =n323=, and =n324= for activation, and
=drake-expiry-n3=, =n4=, =n72=, and =n73= for the next-turn transition.

* General reproduction and evidence

External artifacts are in =/tmp/aw2-weather-power-research/=. =findings.json=
contains the eleven baseline sample frames, source metadata,
full-RAM/screenshot hashes, raw player records, validation decisions,
threshold calculations, and excluded attempts. The separate
=olaf-weather-trace.json= and =drake-weather-trace.json= contain their sequence
provenance and frame boundaries, including the source hashes at capture time.
The evidence manifest fingerprints the retained RAM and screenshot samples.

- Run =python /tmp/aw2-weather-power-research/analyze.py= from the repository
  environment to reproduce the offline field comparisons and assertions.
- The retained Andy/Max inputs are in
  =/tmp/aw2-lifecycle-coverage-research/=, with source hashes and trace inputs
  recorded in that study's metadata and validation matrix.
- For the new Sami activation, restore =super-source.ss0= from that folder,
  open Blue's command menu, choose End, and wait until Red control settles.
  Red is Sami (battle CO ID 4). Open the command menu, choose Power, confirm,
  and wait through the effects. The exact key/frame pairs and each generated
  source checkpoint hash are recorded in =red-ready.json=,
  =sami-power-menu.json=, and =sami-power-settled.json=.
- For weather, restore
  =/tmp/aw2-lifecycle-control-research/sources/aw2.ss3=. Move from Fog to Weather
  with Right, choose Clear or Random using Down, then confirm the Rules screen
  and advance 480 neutral frames. The source and generated checkpoint chains
  are in the =weather-choice-*=, =clear-battle-start.json=, and
  =random-battle-start.json= records. Their PNGs are the manual weather evidence.
- ROM listings use an ELF wrapper generated by =make_elf.py= and the pinned
  LLVM =llvm-objdump --triple=thumbv4t= so literal-pool data cannot shift later
  instruction addresses. The early streaming-disassembly experiment is not
  used as evidence.
