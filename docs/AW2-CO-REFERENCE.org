#+TITLE: Advance Wars 2 CO Reference

Reference summary of the Commanding Officers (COs), their passive strengths and
weaknesses, CO Powers, and Super CO Powers. This is gameplay reference material,
not a verified RAM map. The effects below summarize the
[[https://strategywiki.org/wiki/Advance_Wars_2:_Black_Hole_Rising/COs][StrategyWiki CO guide]] (accessed 2026-10-25); numeric effects and edge cases have
not all been independently tested against the supported US revision-0 ROM.
Where a supplied save-state experiment provides evidence, it is identified
separately.

* General power notes from the guide
- Each CO has a normal CO Power and a stronger Super CO Power, except Sturm,
  who has only Meteor Strike. The guide expresses power cost in stars.
- The guide says power bonuses are additive and generally last until the
  player's next turn. It also describes the power meter as being charged by
  damage taken and, at half rate, damage dealt; using a power raises the next
  cost while carrying unused meter forward. Treat these as guide-reported
  mechanics, not findings from this project's memory research.
- The guide notes a general 10% defense effect when using a power unless a
  different defense effect is specified. This reference does not attempt to
  reconcile that statement with every individual power or game situation.

* Orange Star

** Andy
- Passive: balanced units with no listed attack bonus or penalty.
- CO Power, Hyper Repair (3 stars): heals each unit by 2 HP.
- Super CO Power, Hyper Upgrade (6 stars): heals each unit by 5 HP, gives +1
  movement, and +20% attack for the turn.

** Max
- Passive: direct combat units other than Infantry and Mechs have +20% attack;
  indirect units have -10% attack and one less maximum range.
- CO Power, Max Force (3 stars): eligible direct units get +20% attack and +1
  movement.
- Super CO Power, Max Blast (6 stars): eligible direct units get +40% attack
  and +2 movement.

** Sami
- Passive: Infantry and Mechs have +30% attack and capture at 1.5 times the
  normal rate. APCs, Landers, and T-Copters get +1 movement; other direct units
  have -10% attack.
- CO Power, Double Time (3 stars): Infantry and Mechs get +20% attack and +1
  movement.
- Super CO Power, Victory March (8 stars): Infantry and Mechs get +50% attack
  and +2 movement, and can capture properties regardless of HP. The guide
  describes the capture as instant and includes HQs. The supplied research
  checkpoints show Sami capturing cities with both an Infantry and a Mech
  during the active power; the user confirms a city can be captured in one turn
  regardless of HP. HQ capture under this power has not been isolated in our
  save-state tests. See [[file:AW2-MAP-RESEARCH.org::*Sami Super CO Power captures with Infantry and Mech][the RAM comparison]].

** Nell
- Passive: luck can increase attack damage by up to 10% according to the guide.
- CO Power, Lucky Star (3 stars): attacks can gain up to 6 additional damage
  points.
- Super CO Power, Lady Luck (6 stars): attacks can gain up to 10 additional
  damage points.

** Hachi
- Passive: units retain normal stats and cost 10% less.
- CO Power, Barter (3 stars): the guide says unit prices are reduced and units
  gain +10% defense; its wording conflicts on the discount amount (40% off vs.
  half the original price), so the exact discount needs verification.
- Super CO Power, Merchant Union (5 stars): discounts unit prices, allows land
  units to be bought and deployed from cities, and gives +10% defense. The
  guide has the same discount wording inconsistency as for Barter.

* Blue Moon

** Olaf
- Passive: unaffected by Snow movement penalties; Rain affects his units as
  Snow affects other COs.
- CO Power, Blizzard (3 stars): changes weather to Snow until his next turn.
- Super CO Power, Winter Fury (7 stars): deals 2 HP damage to enemy units and
  changes weather to Snow until his next turn.

** Grit
- Passive: indirect units have +20% attack and +1 maximum range; direct units
  other than Infantry and Mechs have -20% attack.
- CO Power, Snipe Attack (3 stars): indirect units get +30% attack and +1
  maximum range.
- Super CO Power, Super Snipe (6 stars): indirect units get +30% attack and +2
  maximum range.

** Colin
- Passive: units cost 20% less and have -10% attack.
- CO Power, Gold Rush (2 stars): multiplies current funds by 1.5.
- Super CO Power, Power of Money (6 stars): gives +3.33% attack per 1,000
  funds held when activated; funds are not spent.

* Green Earth

** Eagle
- Passive: Air units have +15% attack, +10% defense, and consume 2 less fuel
  per day; Sea units have -30% attack.
- CO Power, Lightning Drive (3 stars): Air units gain another +15% attack and
  +20% defense.
- Super CO Power, Lightning Strike (9 stars): eligible units that already
  acted can act again (Infantry and Mechs are excluded); Air units gain +15%
  attack and +20% defense. The guide also notes a penalty on units the
  following turn but does not give its amount in this summary.

** Drake
- Passive: Sea units have +10% defense and +1 movement; his units avoid Rain
  movement penalties, and his presence makes Rain more likely. Air units have
  -30% attack.
- CO Power, Tsunami (4 stars): deals 1 HP damage to enemy units and halves
  their remaining fuel.
- Super CO Power, Typhoon (7 stars): deals 2 HP damage to enemy units, halves
  their remaining fuel, and changes weather to Rain until his next turn.

** Jess
- Passive: land vehicles other than Infantry and Mechs have +10% attack;
  Air, Sea, Infantry, and Mech units have -10% attack.
- CO Power, Turbo Charge (3 stars): land vehicles get +20% attack and +1
  movement; all units refill fuel and ammo.
- Super CO Power, Overdrive (6 stars): land vehicles get +40% attack and +2
  movement; all units refill fuel and ammo.

* Yellow Comet

** Kanbei
- Passive: all units have +30% attack and +30% defense, and cost 20% more.
- CO Power, Morale Boost (4 stars): units get +20% attack and +10% defense.
- Super CO Power, Samurai Spirit (7 stars): units get +20% attack and +30%
  defense, and a further +50% attack when counterattacking.

** Sonja
- Passive: units get +1 vision and a +50% counterattack bonus; opponents cannot
  see their HP (the guide says this does not affect AI). Her units may also
  deal up to 10% less damage.
- CO Power, Enhanced Vision (3 stars): gives another +1 vision and lets units
  see into Forests and Reefs without adjacent vision.
- Super CO Power, Counter Break (5 stars): has Enhanced Vision's effects and
  lets Sonja's units counterattack before the attacker while the power is
  active.

** Sensei
- Passive: Infantry and Mechs have +40% attack; Battle Copters have +50%;
  APCs, Landers, and T-Copters get +1 movement. Other Land and Sea units have
  -10% attack.
- CO Power, Copter Command (2 stars): creates 9 HP Infantry on eligible empty
  owned cities; the new units can move immediately. Battle Copters get +25%
  attack.
- Super CO Power, Airborne Assault (6 stars): creates 9 HP Mechs on eligible
  empty owned cities; the new units can move immediately. Battle Copters get
  +25% attack.

* Black Hole

** Flak
- Passive: attack has random variation, with possible outcomes ranging from
  an attack increase up to 25% to a decrease up to 10%.
- CO Power, Brute Force (3 stars): random attack damage can gain up to 4 HP or
  lose up to 2 HP.
- Super CO Power, Barbaric Blow (6 stars): random attack damage can gain up to
  7 HP or lose up to 3 HP.

** Lash
- Passive: terrain defense bonuses also increase attack by the same amount.
- CO Power, Terrain Tactics (4 stars): units pay 1 movement point for each
  terrain type they can normally traverse.
- Super CO Power, Prime Tactics (7 stars): has Terrain Tactics' movement
  effect and doubles Lash's terrain-based bonuses.

** Adder
- Passive: no listed attack or defense bonuses; the guide describes his CO
  meter as shorter than average.
- CO Power, Sideslip (2 stars): gives all units +1 movement.
- Super CO Power, Sidewinder (5 stars): gives all units +2 movement.

** Hawke
- Passive: all units have +10% attack.
- CO Power, Black Wave (5 stars): deals 1 HP damage to enemies and heals
  Hawke's units by 1 HP.
- Super CO Power, Black Storm (9 stars): deals 2 HP damage to enemies and
  heals Hawke's units by 2 HP.

** Sturm
- Passive: all units have +20% attack and +20% defense with no added cost;
  units pay 1 movement point for traversable terrain except during Snow.
- Super CO Power only, Meteor Strike (10 stars): drops a meteor on a visible
  area selected for high enemy damage. Units hit lose 8 HP, but are left at
  1 HP if they had 8 HP or less; Sturm's units also gain +20% attack and +30%
  defense. The guide notes targeting constraints intended to avoid striking
  allies when that would cause more damage than the enemy hit.

* Research status and source caveats
- No CO behavior or power field is inferred from the descriptions above. The
  existing RAM work verifies settled-battle CO IDs and selected map/unit fields;
  it does not decode every passive modifier or power state.
- Sami's normal capture multiplier is consistent with the supplied full-HP
  capture checkpoints (15 progress points). Her Super CO Power one-turn city
  capture is user-confirmed and illustrated by the supplied two-city sequence.
- Hachi's price-reduction wording is internally inconsistent in the StrategyWiki
  article. Other exact values and edge cases should be treated as source claims
  until reproduced or verified against the supported ROM.
